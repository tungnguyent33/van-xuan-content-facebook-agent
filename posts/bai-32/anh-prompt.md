# Prompt ảnh Gemini — Bài 32 (Infographic checklist hồ sơ)

Dán nguyên khối dưới đây vào Gemini (hoặc app tạo ảnh AI khác). Sau khi tải ảnh về lưu vào
`posts/bai-32/`, chạy `node scripts/apply-watermark.js posts/bai-32/` để ghép logo Vạn Xuân.

```
Create a modern Vietnamese driving-school announcement infographic, portrait orientation
(4:5), bold and trustworthy, not playful.

Style: modern Vietnamese driving-school ad graphic. Deep navy blue (#0E3970) to vivid blue
(#048DD1) gradient background for the top section, orange accent (#E4661F) for callout
badges and the CTA/contact bar, white for text panels and card backgrounds.

Typography: extra-bold condensed sans-serif, all caps for the headline, white with a subtle
bevel/drop-shadow so it pops on the navy/blue gradient. Use a bold white-on-orange rounded
badge for the key phrase "HẠNG A1 · A".

Headline text (Vietnamese, keep exact wording): "TIẾP NHẬN HỒ SƠ THÁNG 10"
Sub-badge text: "HẠNG A1 · A"

Layout: top section is the navy/blue gradient banner with the headline and sub-badge, a
faint looping road-line motif at low opacity in the background (subtle, not distracting).
Below it, a white card area listing a 4-item checklist with orange circular icons/checkmarks
next to each line of Vietnamese text:
1. CCCD/CMND còn hiệu lực
2. Giấy khám sức khỏe theo mẫu quy định
3. Ảnh thẻ 3x4 nền trắng
4. Đơn đăng ký học lái xe

Below the checklist, a small orange rounded note bar with the text: "Điều kiện: đủ 18 tuổi
trở lên" in bold white text.

Bottom of the image: an orange contact bar (matching the brand CTA bar) with a phone icon
and the text "0823279999" plus a Zalo icon, on a solid orange background spanning the width.

Logo placement: reserve clean, empty space in the top-left corner for overlaying the
official Vạn Xuân logo — do not generate any text or icons in top-left area.

Overall mood: clean, organized, easy to scan on a phone screen, consistent with a
professional driving-school brand — avoid clip-art style icons, avoid overly playful colors
outside the navy/blue/orange/white palette.
```
